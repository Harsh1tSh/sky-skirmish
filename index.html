How to play

Your jetpack refuels on the ground. Your health comes back after 5 seconds without being hit. Crouching steadies your aim, and headshots hit harder.

MoveA D
FlyW or Space
Crouch, or drop through a platformS
AimMouse, or arrow keys
ShootLeft click, or J
ZoomHold right click or Z, or toggle with V
MeleeF or K
Throw grenadeG or L
Change grenade, gas or mineT
ReloadR
Switch weaponQ, mouse wheel, 1 2
Swap for the gun at your feetE
ScoreboardTab (hold)
Pause / MuteEsc / M

Gamepads work too: left stick moves, right stick aims, right trigger shoots. On a phone, use the on-screen sticks.
